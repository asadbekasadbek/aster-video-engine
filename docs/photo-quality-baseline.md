## Implementation and reproduction

Rust CLI `quality --image PATH` uses installed native FFmpeg to decode one RGB
image, Lanczos aspect-fill/center-crop to704x384, normalize to[-1,1], then the
actual BF16 VAE encoder/posterior mode/per-channel normalization. The conditioned
first latent plane has264tokens, fixed at noise timestep0 and restored exactly
after every Euler step. All remaining latent frames are generated by seven real
DiT forwards (7392processedtokens); frozen-token error0. This is not a slideshow,
pan/zoom effect, copied RGB first frame or a postprocess sharpening filter.
All25outputframes, including the first, come through the VAE decoder.

Existing fixed384x256 reference wrappers are preserved. New bounded dimension
adapters accept only384x256,576x384,704x384; input shape/extent/range conversion is
tested. `reference704-fullvae` disables temporal splitting for this short clip,
while retaining spatial tiling512. It requires an image and >=10240MiB free GPU
memory on admission. This guard is conservative, not total CUDA memory accounting.

```powershell
.\target\release\aster.exe quality --prompt-file examples/quality-reference.txt --output artifacts/my-photo-video.mp4 --worker target/ltx/release/aster-ltx.exe --models models/ltx-0.9.8-2b --ffmpeg C:\OSPanel\modules\php\php_8.2\ffmpeg.exe --profile reference704-fullvae --seed 42 --image artifacts/quality-reference/source.jpg
```

Exact prompt and parameters are in the `.params.txt` sidecar; sampled VRAM in
the `.log`. Existing approved2B0.9.8distilledBF16, Q5_K_M CPU T5 with256tokens,
CFG1/STG0, seven explicit published first-pass sigmas, normalized-space decoder
noise. No multi-scale/upscaler or13B claim. Prompt requests subtle breathing/finger
motion, stable camera and preservation of appearance. The input is a reference
for this experiment only; no training or general identity embedding was added.

## Visual findings and acceptance

Compared frames0,6,12,18,24 for all three; native-resolution frame12 of both seeds
and full-temporal decode; all25frames of full decode in `fullvae-all25.png`.

- Reference conditioning retains the recognizable man, yellow jacket, car and
  lighting much better than the earlier unrelated text-only outputs. Different
  input/prompt makes this an observation, not a paired text-only quality score.
- Both split-decode trials visibly smear/double edges near the middle of the
  clip. Seed43 also changes face pose more substantially. Neither meets the target.
- Same-seed42 full-temporal decode visibly reduces the middle-frame double edges
  and restores clearer facial/clothing contours. This isolates a decode setting,
  but does not prove complete VAE parity or identify the exact low-level blending
  defect. It is the selected candidate to show the user.
- Small head/hand/body changes are visible: motion is not merely repeating the
  source. However the scene reframes and fine water/skin/fabric texture still
  softens. No claim of anatomically perfect fingers, identical detail, zero
  morphing, or target-quality completion. Human quality approval remains open.
